### Discussion & Reflection Prompts

1. Where in our current continuous-improvement work are we still asking “How do we do this with fewer or cheaper hours?” instead of “How do we strengthen American productive capacity?”

2. Which steps in our major value streams currently depend on short-cycle non-sovereign labor to function?

3. If we redefined “waste” to include loss of institutional knowledge and “value” to include high-skill American roles, which improvement projects would look different?

4. How often do our Lean or value-stream efforts create higher-skill domestic work versus simply removing hours?

5. What would a value-stream redesign look like if the primary goal was to expand higher-skill American employment created by process or technology innovation?

6. How tightly are our current improvement efforts connected to the Business Model Canvas, OKRs, and Operating Model updates from the earlier modules?
