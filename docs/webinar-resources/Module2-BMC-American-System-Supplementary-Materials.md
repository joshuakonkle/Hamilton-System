### Discussion & Reflection Prompts

1. Where in our current OKR system are we still treating the goals and the system that drives them as the same thing?

2. Which of our top-level Objectives, if achieved, would actually strengthen domestic productive capacity and high-skill American roles? Which would mainly reduce cost through labor flexibility?

3. If we rewrote one major Key Result so it measured capability growth instead of headcount cost or speed-to-fill, what would change in how teams behave?

4. How exposed is our current OKR system to the risk that we are optimizing for short-term labor arbitrage while eroding long-term institutional knowledge?

5. What would an OKR look like that deliberately rewards the shift of American workers into higher-skill roles created by process or technology innovation?

6. How tightly are our current OKRs connected to the Business Model Canvas updates we discussed in Module 1?

### 90-Day Action Plan Outline

This is a high-level structure only. A complete, customized 90-day execution plan is available as a paid implementation offering.

**Days 1–30: Diagnose & Align**
- Complete the OKR System Diagnostic with the leadership team
- Inventory current top-level Objectives and Key Results
- Flag those that still optimize for labor arbitrage or pure cost extraction
- Select one major OKR for immediate rewrite

**Days 31–60: Redesign**
- Rewrite the selected Objective and its Key Results so they drive productive sovereignty
- Define new success metrics focused on capability growth and domestic strength
- Align the rewritten OKR with the updated Business Model Canvas (Module 1)
- Socialize the change with the relevant teams

**Days 61–90: Execute & Embed**
- Launch the rewritten OKR into the active cycle
- Establish a simple monthly review on the new metrics
- Identify the next 1–2 OKRs to bring into alignment
- Document lessons and decide next 90-day priorities
