### Discussion & Reflection Prompts

1. Where in our current workforce strategy are we still treating roles as a filling problem rather than a capability-building problem?

2. Looking at our most critical technical or skilled roles, what percentage are currently filled by long-term American workers versus short-cycle non-sovereign labor?

3. If we treated the development of American talent as a strategic investment in productive capacity instead of a cost to be minimized, which pipelines would we start or expand first?

4. How exposed are we to the quiet erosion of institutional knowledge and innovation capacity caused by repeated temporary-labor rotations?

5. What would a 3–5 year American talent pipeline for one critical role family actually need to include (sources, development steps, retention logic, success metrics)?

6. How tightly is our workforce strategy connected to the Business Model Canvas, OKRs, Operating Model, and Value Stream work already completed?

### 90-Day Action Plan Outline

- Complete the Sovereign Workforce Diagnostic with the leadership team

**Days 61–90: Execute & Embed**
- Launch the first concrete pipeline action
- Establish a simple monthly review on capability and dependence metrics
- Capture early lessons and adjust
