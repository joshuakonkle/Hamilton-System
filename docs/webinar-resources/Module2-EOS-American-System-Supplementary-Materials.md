## 1. Diagnostic Checklist

Use with leadership or alone. Prefer facts over aspirations. Complete in the **guided checklist** (Yes / Partially / No + short note). No essay required.

### Rocks as a system (not only a goal list)
- [ ] Leadership treats **Rocks as a system** that drives weekly focus and accountability — not only a list of annual wishes.
- [ ] Rocks are few, owned, and reviewed in Level 10; unfinished Rocks are IDS’d, not quietly dropped.

### Capacity-linked Rocks
- [ ] At least one Rock is explicitly tied to **building capacity** (pipeline, knowledge retention, critical seat strength, process that elevates work) — not only revenue, fill-rate, or cost cut.
- [ ] Capacity Rocks have a clear owner who can affect the outcome (not only HR or Finance as spectators).

### Short-cycle temporary or visa labor
- [ ] No Rock assumes **permanent** short-cycle temporary or visa labor coverage as the way to hit the number.
- [ ] **Failure mode check:** A Rock that can only be hit by expanding permanent short-cycle temporary or visa labor design on critical seats is treated as a **capacity risk**, not a win.

### Hand-off to Scorecard & People
- [ ] Capacity Rocks point to a metric that could live on the Scorecard (Module 3) and to seats that Module 5 would protect.
- [ ] When a capacity Rock is red and cost is green, Level 10 still IDS’s it.

### Anchors (shared language)
- [ ] Team can state: Rocks are the system that turns Vision into weekly execution; capacity Rocks keep the system from optimizing cost while strength erodes.
- [ ] **Posture:** Operating in the United States is not workforce-neutral. Seat design either builds lasting productive capacity or rents weakness.

**Diagnostic result (one line):**  
Where is the gap — Rocks as a wish list, no capacity-linked Rock, Rocks that depend on permanent short-cycle temporary or visa labor design, or Level 10 that skips capacity when cost is green?

## 2. 90-Day Outline (Free Execution Scaffold)

This is a **starting outline**, not a full managed engagement. Paid implementation support (e.g. 90-Day Sovereign Capacity Sprint) sits behind deeper facilitation and cross-module integration.

### Days 1–30 — Diagnose
| Action | Output |
|--------|--------|
| List current Rocks; mark which are capacity-linked vs cost/fill only | Tagged Rock list |
| Test whether any Rock depends on permanent short-cycle temporary or visa labor design | Failure-mode notes |
| Confirm Rocks are owned, few, and actually reviewed in Level 10 | System health notes |
| Map capacity Rocks to a future Scorecard metric and to critical seats | Hand-off sketch |

### Days 31–60 — Redesign
| Action | Output |
|--------|--------|
| Add or rewrite **one** Rock that builds capacity (pipeline, knowledge, critical seat, elevated process) | Rock statement + **operator** owner + due |
| Strip or reframe any Rock that only wins by expanding permanent short-cycle temporary or visa labor coverage on critical seats | Revised Rock set |
| Link the capacity Rock to a metric name (Module 3) and seat family (Module 5) | Metric + seat placeholders |

### Days 61–90 — Execute & Embed
| Action | Output |
|--------|--------|
| Run Level 10s that IDS capacity Rocks when they are red even if cost is green | Documented IDS examples |
| Keep the capacity Rock visible in the next quarter’s set | Locked Rocks list |
| Hand off to Scorecard (Module 3) and People (Module 5) | Short hand-off list |

**Success check at day 90:**  
Rocks are run as a system (owned, few, reviewed); at least one Rock is capacity-linked with an operator owner; no Rock depends on unchallenged permanent short-cycle temporary or visa labor design for critical seats — **so Rocks build lasting productive capacity rather than rent weakness.**
