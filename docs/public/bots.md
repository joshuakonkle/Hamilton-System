# Bots

Four seats · not a starter pack

The same four seats as Become an Operator. This page shows a public reference for each seat. It is not five things to buy, and you do not stand all four up at once.

## 1. Primary Bot

Start here when you want help turning a task into finished work.

Grok Bot is an AI teammate that finishes real work by signing into your tools, using them like you, and returning completed tasks. Bots can research, draft, and collaborate in parallel while learning from the workflows and context you give them.

[x.ai/bot](https://x.ai/bot)

## 2. Scaffold Bot

When you need a bot for a specific job, this helps you define its responsibilities and set it up.

Dr. EggBot designs high-quality Grok Bots by asking preference questions and using CreateAgent. It creates coding bots with one job, unslopped and verified, and non-coding bots with one job, one voice, explicit anti-jobs, and no leftover tools.

[Dr. EggBot](https://x.ai/bot/marketplace/bots/dr-eggbot-v2)

## 3. PM/QA Bot

When several bots are working together, this helps keep work coordinated and checks that the results meet your requirements.

The engineering guide describes a fleet with a head of operations who manages onboarding, daily meetings, postmortems, and alignment, plus engineer bots that review code and verify work. No seat approves its own work.

[Grok Bot for engineering](https://x.ai/bot/guides/grok-bot-for-engineering)

## 4. Working Bot

Give a bot one recurring job—Tony shows how that works for a job seeker monitoring PERM hiring.

Tony, the PERM Watch Bot. Runs a weekly PERM labor-market test for a qualified U.S. job seeker. It applies to PERM ads, flags employers who stay silent past 24 hours, drafts the silence post, preps regulator filings, with you approving every send.

[Get Tony](https://x.ai/bot/g0SYDSP-RNIQBp31kMKEK)
