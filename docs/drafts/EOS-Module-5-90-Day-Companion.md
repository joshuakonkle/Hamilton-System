### Discussion & Reflection Prompts

1. Where are we still treating seats as a filling problem rather than a capability-building problem?
2. For our most critical technical seats, what percentage are held by long-term American workers versus short-cycle non-sovereign labor?
3. If we treated American talent development as a strategic investment in productive capacity, which Rock would we set this quarter?
4. How exposed are we to quiet erosion of institutional knowledge from repeated temporary-labor rotations?
5. What would a 3–5 year American talent pipeline for one critical seat family need to include (sources, development steps, retention logic, Scorecard metrics)?
6. Does our V/TO long-range picture still assume continuous low-cost temporary labor — and if so, what changes?

### 90-Day Action Plan Outline (EOS cadence)

This is a high-level structure only. A complete, customized 90-day execution plan is available as a paid implementation offering.

**Days 1–30 – Diagnose (inside existing EOS rhythm)**
- Complete the Sovereign Workforce Diagnostic with the leadership team
- Identify the two or three seat families most critical to long-term productive capacity
- Measure current dependence on temporary or short-cycle non-sovereign labor for those seats
- Select one seat family for focused pipeline design
- Review current Rocks and Scorecard for that family (cost/fill vs. capability)

**Days 31–60 – Redesign**
- Rewrite 1–2 Rocks so they explicitly build sovereign capability in the selected seat family
- Add 2–3 Scorecard metrics that track pipeline health and retention of institutional knowledge (same language as BMC Module 5 diagnostic)
- For that seat family: run standard GWC, then the additional American System capacity question on critical seats
- Align the redesign with V/TO (10-Year Target / 3-Year Picture) so the long-range picture does not assume permanent temp dependence

**Days 61–90 – Execute & Embed**
- Launch the new Rocks and Scorecard measures
- Run at least one Level 10 Meeting cycle that IDS’s a concrete pipeline obstacle
- Capture early lessons and adjust
- Decide the next seat family to bring into the same discipline
