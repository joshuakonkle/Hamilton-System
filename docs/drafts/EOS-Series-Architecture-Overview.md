# EOS Series – High-Level Architecture (Draft for Review)
**Modules 1–4 Overview + Module 5 Context**

Purpose of this note: Give an EOS-certified reviewer the intended shape of the full five-module series so Module 5 (Workforce) can be read in context.

---

### Series Goal
Re-orient the core EOS tools through a Hamiltonian / American System lens:
- Productive sovereignty
- High-skill American capability as the foundation of capacity
- Innovation and directed improvement over labor arbitrage
- Permanent institutional knowledge rather than short-cycle temporary labor

The free BMC series already exists. The EOS series follows the same modular logic but maps onto native EOS language and rhythm.

---

### Module 1 – Vision/Traction Organizer (V/TO)
**EOS Core Tool:** Vision/Traction Organizer  
**Hamiltonian Filter:** Does the 10-Year Target, 3-Year Picture, and 1-Year Plan explicitly build domestic productive strength and high-skill American roles, or does it still assume continuous access to low-cost temporary labor?

Key move: Rewrite Core Focus / 10-Year Target language so productive capacity and sovereign workforce are visible design criteria, not afterthoughts.

---

### Module 2 – Rocks & Scorecard
**EOS Core Tools:** Rocks + Scorecard  
**Hamiltonian Filter:** Are Rocks building capability or merely filling seats? Do Scorecard measurables track sovereign workforce health and institutional knowledge, or only cost and activity?

Key move: Convert at least some Rocks from pure activity/cost targets into capability-building Rocks. Add a small set of leading indicators for pipeline health and knowledge retention.

---

### Module 3 – Process Component + Right People / Right Seats
**EOS Core Tools:** Process documentation + People Analyzer / GWC  
**Hamiltonian Filter:** When processes are simplified or automated, do the remaining seats elevate American workers into higher-skill design, maintenance, and improvement roles? Are seats designed so knowledge compounds rather than resets with every rotation?

Key move: Treat process clarity and seat design as the mechanism that protects and grows institutional knowledge.

---

### Module 4 – Level 10 Meetings + IDS (Identify/Discuss/Solve)
**EOS Core Tools:** Level 10 Meeting rhythm + IDS  
**Hamiltonian Filter:** Is the weekly meeting used only to clear issues, or is it also used to surface and solve systemic capability and pipeline problems?

Key move: Expand the IDS lens so loss of institutional knowledge and over-reliance on short-cycle labor are treated as legitimate issues to be solved, not accepted operating conditions.

---

### Module 5 – Workforce Strategy (Full Draft Provided Separately)
**EOS Core Tools:** People Analyzer, GWC, Seat design, Rocks & Scorecard applied to talent  
**Role in the series:** Kingpin module. Once the workforce logic is clear, Modules 1–4 are rewritten so every tool reinforces the same productive-sovereignty filter.

---

### Review Request
- Does the overall mapping of Hamiltonian principles onto native EOS tools feel coherent?
- Any Module 1–4 framing that would create friction for an EOS-trained leadership team?
- Guidance on sequencing or emphasis before the full drafts of Modules 1–4 are written.

Thank you for the cursory review.
