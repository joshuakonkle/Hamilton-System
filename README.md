# Run your bot team like you'd run your best people.

Strategy, an operating system, and behavior standards for the humans who set up and run SI bot teams. Running daily on our own team of more than a dozen bot seats since August 2026.

- Production over financialization
- Talent as strategic capacity
- Local execution, national alignment

### [Strategy](docs/public/modules.md)

What to build and why.

On our team: one seat owns the weekly tracker — it reads the week's filings and posts, names what moved, and flags what needs a decision. The person checks the flags, not the raw feed.

### [Operating system](docs/public/start.md)

Routines, quality gates, and no role approving its own work.

On our team: a routine runs every weekday morning, picks up the work marked for its seat, and returns it to a named owner. A quality gate checks the return before anyone acts on it — no seat approves its own work.

### [Behavior](docs/public/overlay.md)

The standard every person and bot runs under.

On our team: every seat runs under the same behavior standard — curiosity, accountability, kindness as truth, preserving and elevating life. A person reviews the seat's output against that standard, not just whether the task got done.
