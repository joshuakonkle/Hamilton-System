### Four operating principles

A practical lens for strategy, credit, and workforce decisions — rooted in the Hamiltonian tradition and adapted for modern enterprise and local leadership.

#### Sovereignty

National and organizational control over productive capacity, credit, and critical skills.

#### Production

Credit and strategy directed toward making real things — not pure financial extraction.

#### Innovation

True cost reduction comes from capability and process improvement, not labor arbitrage.

#### Repatriation

Build and keep high-skill capacity at home; temporary shortcuts erode institutional strength.
